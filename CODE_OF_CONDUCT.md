# Code of Conduct

Darb is an open-source project for people who want to learn at their own pace.
We want the project to be useful, welcoming, and safe to contribute to.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code or ideas.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful behavior directly.
- Welcome contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Keep feedback focused on the work, with clear suggestions where possible.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through the security
contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed as
promptly and confidentially as possible. Maintainers may remove content or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This policy applies to the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, project
discussions, and public spaces where someone represents Darb.
